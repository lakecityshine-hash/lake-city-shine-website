COMMON QUESTIONS

Planning your carpet cleaning

Can I request only carpet cleaning?

Yes. Choose Professional Carpet Cleaning / Upholstery as the main service on the quote form. You can also select carpet cleaning as an add-on to an eligible cleaning service.

What does carpet cleaning cost?

We quote according to the area, condition, stairs, access and agreed work. Share your room count or approximate carpet area so we can review your request.

How long will the carpet take to dry?

Drying varies with the carpet, condition, airflow and work required. Ask about preparation, drying and when the rooms can be used as part of your quote.
