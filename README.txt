Lake City Shine local SEO and AI search update

This package updates 10 existing HTML pages and robots.txt. It improves unique page titles and descriptions, Open Graph metadata, matching structured-data text, local headings on relevant pages, and a short service-specific explanatory sentence in existing page copy. It preserves the layout, styling, and navigation.

Included pages: index.html, residential.html, move-in-out.html, deep-cleaning.html, commercial.html, turnovers.html, post-construction.html, pressure-washing.html, window-cleaning.html, exterior.html, plus robots.txt.

AI/search crawler access:
- robots.txt explicitly permits Googlebot and Google-Extended (Google Search/Gemini-related access), Bingbot (including Microsoft search experiences), OAI-SearchBot and ChatGPT-User, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, and Applebot.
- This grants crawl permission; it does not force an AI platform to index, mention, cite, or recommend the business. Search platform behavior and eligibility vary.
- No special AI schema, hidden keyword list, or llms.txt file is required for Google AI features. The pages need to be crawlable, indexable, helpful, and consistent with the business profile.

Apply:
1. Back up the current repository.
2. Replace the matching root files with the package versions.
3. Commit and publish the site.
4. In Google Search Console, inspect key service URLs, request indexing, and keep the sitemap submitted.
5. In Bing Webmaster Tools, verify the domain and submit the sitemap.
6. Check that the Google Business Profile is complete and accurate: primary category, services, service areas, hours, phone, website, and photos. Ask customers for honest reviews and respond to them. Local ranking also depends on distance and prominence, which website edits cannot control.
7. Track queries and conversions in Search Console and Analytics after recrawling. Google may take days to weeks to process title changes; indexing and ranking are not guaranteed.

Keyword phrases are selected around actual services and service areas rather than every conceivable wording. Google ignores the meta-keywords tag and warns against keyword stuffing.
