Deep House Cleaning for Kitchens, Bathrooms & More

Deep cleaning focuses on built-up dirt, grime and overlooked areas throughout the home. We spend extra time on kitchens, bathrooms, floors, trim, appliances and high-touch areas to give the property a more complete reset.

Kitchen Deep Cleaning

Clean counters, sinks, backsplash and cabinet exteriors, with extra attention to grease and buildup.

Bathroom Deep Cleaning

Clean tubs, showers, toilets, sinks, mirrors and fixtures, including areas affected by soap scum and buildup.

Interior Windows

Clean accessible interior glass and sills to remove dust, fingerprints and residue.

Walls & Baseboards

Spot-clean wall marks and baseboards as part of the agreed scope. Full wall washing is available as an additional service.

Floors & Detail Work

Vacuum and mop floors and clean doors, handles, switches and other commonly touched surfaces.

Appliance Interiors

Include refrigerator, freezer and oven interiors in your quote for a more complete kitchen clean.

COMMON QUESTIONS

A few things you may be wondering

How is a deep clean different from routine upkeep?

A deep clean allows more attention to buildup and detail. The rooms, surfaces and priorities are agreed with your quote.

Does deep cleaning include full wall washing?

Full wall washing is a separate service from spot cleaning. Tell us about the surfaces and buildup so we can quote the work you need.

Can I continue with recurring cleaning afterward?

Yes. Ask about weekly, every-two-week or monthly home cleaning, subject to local service coverage and availability.
