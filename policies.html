Professional Conduct

Represent Lake City Shine professionally. Treat customers, coworkers, belongings, and property with respect. Keep customer information private.

Smoking & Vaping

Smoking, vaping, or use of tobacco or nicotine products is not permitted inside any customer's home, business, or other indoor jobsite.

Personal Phone Use

Phones may be used as long as use does not interfere with the work or Lake City Shine quality standards. Personal phone use should be kept to a minimum in the presence of customers. Work-related phone use is permitted.

Customer Payments

Cole handles customer payments. Employees are not authorized to request, collect, process, or arrange payment unless Cole has been contacted first and specifically authorizes it. Employees may not change prices, negotiate invoices, or give discounts without authorization.

Tips

Tips belong to the cleaner who receives them. Cash tips may be accepted but must be reported to Cole so they are identified as tips rather than payment for cleaning services. Electronic tips handled through Lake City Shine will be passed to the appropriate cleaner.

Customer Privacy & Photos

Only take photos necessary for Lake City Shine work. Avoid private documents, screens, financial information, medications, family photographs, and unrelated private information. Do not post job photos to personal social media.

Damage & Existing Conditions

Document and report existing damage. If accidental damage occurs, stop work on the affected item or area and report it immediately. Do not conceal damage or promise payment or replacement.

Chemical Safety

Use approved chemicals and follow labels, SDS information, dilution, ventilation, PPE, and surface-compatibility requirements. Never mix chemicals unless specifically approved. If unsure, stop and ask.

Equipment Safety

Inspect equipment before use. Do not use damaged or unsafe equipment. Report equipment problems.

Quality Control

Perform a final walkthrough, check the applicable checklist, correct missed items, remove company materials, and take required completion photos.

Keys, Codes & Property Access

Customer keys, lockbox codes, alarm codes, door codes, and access information are confidential. Secure the property appropriately when leaving.

Customer Requests & Additional Work

Do not promise significant work outside the scheduled scope or quote prices. Contact management for approval.

Reporting Problems

Report damage, safety concerns, unusual conditions, equipment problems, and chemical or surface concerns. For an immediate safety concern, stop work and contact management.

Cleaning Supplies

Submit a Cleaning Supply Request before supplies are depleted when possible. Do not purchase or substitute unapproved chemicals without authorization.
