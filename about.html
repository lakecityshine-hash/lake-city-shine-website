How Lake City Shine Began

Lake City Shine began with our last $100 and a used carpet cleaner. There wasn't a big budget or a warehouse full of equipment—just the determination to work hard, provide dependable service and build something of our own.

One job led to another. As customers trusted us with their homes and properties, we continued reinvesting into better equipment, supplies and services. What started small has continued to grow into a local cleaning company serving homeowners, businesses, property managers and contractors throughout Northern Indiana.

OUR MISSION

Quality cleaning should still be within reach.

Our mission is to help people throughout our community and provide an affordable cleaning option without sacrificing quality.

Whether we're preparing a home for its next occupant, turning an apartment, cleaning a business, handling post-construction cleanup or taking care of a property that needs extra attention, we want our work to make a real difference for the people we serve.
