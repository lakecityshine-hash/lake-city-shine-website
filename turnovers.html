COMMON QUESTIONS

A few things you may be wondering

Can I request cleaning for several units?

Yes. Share the number of units, sizes, condition and target dates. We will confirm scope and scheduling for the group.

Do you also clean common areas and stairways?

Yes. Common-area and staircase cleaning can be discussed alongside unit turns or as a separate request.

What information helps with a turnover quote?

Unit size, occupancy status, condition, access and the next move-in date help us understand the work. You can provide these after your initial inquiry.
