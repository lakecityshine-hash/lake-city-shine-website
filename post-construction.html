WHAT WE CLEAN

The cleanup after the construction crew is done.

Construction and remodeling can leave fine dust, footprints, residue and debris throughout a property. We focus on getting the finished space cleaned up and ready for the next step—whether that means a homeowner moving back in, a tenant taking possession, or a contractor turning over a completed project.

Construction Dust & Surfaces

Detailed dust removal from accessible surfaces, ledges, trim, doors, fixtures, switches and other areas where fine construction dust settles.

Floors

Vacuuming, mopping and detailed floor cleaning to remove dust, footprints and jobsite residue from finished flooring.

Kitchens & Bathrooms

Cleaning of counters, sinks, tubs, showers, toilets, vanities, cabinets and finished fixtures so newly completed rooms look ready to use.

Windows & Final Details

Interior glass, sills, frames and detail areas are cleaned as part of preparing the property for its final presentation.

BUILT AROUND THE PROJECT

New construction, renovations and remodels.

Not every project needs the same cleanup. We can focus on a specific portion of the property or clean the completed space as a whole based on the condition of the jobsite and the scope you need.

Whole-Property Cleanup

Detailed cleaning throughout completed homes, apartments, offices and other finished spaces.

Floor-Only Cleaning

A focused option when the project mainly needs finished flooring cleaned after construction work.

Renovation Cleanup

Cleaning after remodeling, painting, flooring installation and other improvement projects.

Turnover-Ready Cleaning

For contractors and property teams preparing a completed project for the owner, tenant or next stage.
