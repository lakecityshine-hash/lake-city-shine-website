WHAT WE CLEAN

Post-Construction Dust Removal & Final Cleaning

Construction and remodeling can leave fine dust, footprints, residue and debris throughout a property. We focus on getting the finished space cleaned up and ready for the next step—whether that means a homeowner moving back in, a tenant taking possession, or a contractor turning over a completed project.

Construction Dust & Surfaces

Remove settled construction dust from accessible ledges, trim, doors, fixtures, switches and other finished surfaces.

Floors

Vacuum and mop finished floors to address dust, footprints and jobsite residue using methods suited to the flooring.

Kitchens & Bathrooms

Clean counters, sinks, tubs, showers, toilets, vanities and cabinet surfaces after installation or remodeling.

Windows & Final Details

Detail accessible interior glass, sills and frames. Exterior or elevated glass is quoted according to scope and access.

BUILT AROUND THE PROJECT

New Construction & Renovation Cleanup

Not every project needs the same cleanup. We can focus on a specific portion of the property or clean the completed space as a whole based on the condition of the jobsite and the scope you need.

Whole-Property Cleanup

Final cleaning for completed homes, apartment units, offices and other finished properties before handover.

Floor-Only Cleaning

A focused cleaning scope for finished floors when the rest of the property does not need a full final clean.

Renovation Cleanup

Clean completed areas after remodeling, painting or flooring work, with attention to dust and residue left behind.

Turnover-Ready Cleaning

Prepare finished spaces for an owner walkthrough, new occupant or project handover. Confirm timing around remaining trade work.

COMMON QUESTIONS

A few things you may be wondering

When should I contact you about a project?

Send your project location, approximate size and expected completion date while planning the cleanup. Scheduling is confirmed once the scope and access are understood.

Can you quote a final clean or a smaller part of the project?

Yes. Describe the areas and stage of work. We can discuss final cleaning, renovation cleanup or a more limited scope.

What about windows and hard-to-reach areas?

Include the window count, height and access in your request. We confirm which areas can be included and any access requirements before agreeing to the work.
